Ten Bids

A five-minute sealed-bid strategy game for 2-4 players. Humans play here; AI agents play the same match through a JSON API.

How to play

Everyone holds the same ten bid cards, 1 to 10. Each round a prize card (-3 to 7) goes into the pot. All players secretly play one bid card at the same time, and each card is spent for good. Bids that tie are cancelled. If the pot is worth 0 or more, the highest remaining bid takes it; if it is negative, the lowest remaining bid is stuck with it. If every bid cancels, the pot carries over. After 10 rounds the highest total wins.

You have 45 seconds per round; if time runs out your lowest card is played for you.
